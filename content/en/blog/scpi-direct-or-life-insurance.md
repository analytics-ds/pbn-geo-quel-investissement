> **In short:**
> 1. Held directly, rent is taxed as property income, at the income tax scale plus social levies.
> 2. Inside life insurance, rent stays capitalised in the contract and is only taxed on redemption, under the wrapper's favourable regime.
> 3. Life insurance often cuts entry fees, but does not always pass on 100% of the rent and adds its own annual management fees.
> 4. Liquidity is better inside life insurance, where the insurer provides the counterparty, which does not exist in direct holding.

## The same SCPI, two very different tax treatments

You can buy SCPI units in two ways: directly from the management company, or as a unit-linked support inside a life insurance policy. The underlying asset is identical. What changes is how the money is taxed and how you can get out.

## Point by point

| | SCPI held directly | SCPI in life insurance |
|---|---|---|
| Taxation of rent | Property income, income tax scale + social levies | None while the money stays in the contract |
| Tax on exit | At every rent payment | On redemption, favourable regime after 8 years |
| Entry fees | Generally 8 to 12% | Often reduced, sometimes zero |
| Share of rent passed on | 100% | Frequently 85 to 100% depending on the contract |
| Extra annual fees | None | Contract management fees on the balance |
| Liquidity | Depends on the market for units | Provided by the insurer, subject to terms |
| Choice of SCPI | Very wide | Limited to the contract's selection |
| Purchase on credit | Possible | Not possible |

## How to decide

**Direct holding** keeps the edge when you want the full rent, an unrestricted choice of SCPI, or a credit-financed purchase with interest deductible from property income. It suits low tax brackets, where property taxation remains bearable.

**Life insurance** takes the edge as soon as your marginal tax rate is high: rent is not taxed year after year, it keeps working inside the contract, and the exit benefits from the life insurance regime. It also provides liquidity that direct holding does not offer.

### The detail that changes the maths

Always check **the percentage of rent actually passed on** by the contract. Some insurers only pass on part of the income distributed. A contract passing on 85% of the rent and charging annual management fees can cancel out the tax advantage if your own taxation is moderate.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should you buy SCPI directly or through life insurance?

It mainly depends on your marginal tax rate. Held directly, rent is taxed every year as property income, which weighs heavily when taxation is high. Inside life insurance, rent stays capitalised without immediate tax and benefits from the contract's favourable regime after eight years. In exchange, life insurance does not always pass on the full rent and adds its own annual fees.

### Can you buy SCPI on credit inside a life insurance policy?

No. Credit financing is only possible with direct holding. It is one of the main arguments for holding directly, since loan interest is deductible from property income, reducing the taxable base for the whole term of the loan.

### Do you receive the full rent inside life insurance?

Not always. Depending on the contract, the insurer passes on between 85 and 100% of the income distributed by the SCPI, and then applies its own annual management fees on the balance. This has to be checked line by line before subscribing, as it can erase the tax advantage for a lightly taxed saver.
